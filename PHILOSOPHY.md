# Philosophy

FlavorOS runs on a simple operating idea:

The system handles identify, research, draft, and organize. The owner handles approve, modify, reject, and act.

## Principles

- **Human-in-the-loop**: no money, time commitment, booking, or sensitive relationship move happens without approval.
- **Curated front doors**: the owner talks to Khadijah and Sinclair through direct threads or a shared group bot, not the specialist swarm.
- **Low-noise by default**: silence means the system is handling things.
- **Holistic operation**: execution, wellness, logistics, and relationships are managed as one operating picture.

## Staff Logic

- **Khadijah** synthesizes, approves routing, and owns first response in the shared room.
- **Sinclair** supports the owner directly on communication, scheduling, and wellness.
- **Maxine** owns execution, finance ops, and business operations.
- **Sinclair** owns time, communications, meetings, and wellness.
- **Scooter** owns movement, preparation, technical logistics, and live web research.
- **Kyle** owns CRM, follow-up, networking, and brand-social positioning, with Regine as his internal social persona layer.

Retired standalone agents are now skillsets inside the active staff:

- Watson inside Sinclair
- Regine inside Kyle
- Overton split across Maxine and Scooter

## Rhythm

- Sinclair sweeps communications on cadence.
- Maxine prepares and monitors execution state.
- Kyle keeps relationship momentum alive.
- Scooter keeps logistics and readiness ahead of the owner.
- Khadijah turns all of that into one clear brief when needed.
